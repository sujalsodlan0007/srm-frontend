# SRM GLOBAL HUB - Premium B2B Clothing Manufacturing Website Plan

---

## 1. Company Overview
**SRM GLOBAL HUB** is a professional ladies garments manufacturing company specializing in high-quality, custom clothing for brands, boutiques, and retailers worldwide.

---

## 2. Core Goals of the Website
1. **Manufacturing Showcase**: Display the entire manufacturing process from fabric selection to final packing.
2. **Trust Building**: Establish credibility with factory photos, videos, certifications, and client testimonials.
3. **Easy Client Connection**: Make it effortless for clients to contact, request quotes, and submit designs.
4. **Lead Management**: System to capture, track, and respond to inquiries efficiently.
5. **Premium Branding**: Position SRM GLOBAL HUB as a reliable, professional manufacturing partner.

## 5. Design & Branding Guidelines
### 5.1 Color Scheme
- Primary: Deep Navy Blue (#0A192F) - Professional, Trustworthy
- Secondary: Gold/Yellow (#F59E0B) - Premium, Attention-grabbing
- Accent: White/Gray - Clean, Modern
- Avoid bright, "cheap" colors

### 5.2 Typography
- Headings: Poppins (Bold)
- Body: Inter (Regular/Medium)
- Keep it clean and readable

### 5.3 Imagery
- All photos must be high-quality (1920x1080 minimum)
- Use real photos of your factory, team, and products
- Avoid stock images that don't look authentic
- Use consistent lighting and style

### 5.4 Tone of Voice
- Professional, friendly, and trustworthy
- Use simple, clear language
- Avoid jargon
- Focus on benefits for the client

## 10. Additional Recommendations
1. **Get Professional Photos/Videos**: Invest in a good photographer/videographer for authentic content.
2. **Start with Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**: Launch the core pages first, then add more features later.
3. **Add Live Chat**: Consider adding a live chat widget for real-time client support.
4. **Social Media Integration**: Link to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn to showcase your work.
5. **Case Studies**: Add detailed case studies of past projects with photos and client feedback.
6. **Blog**: Share manufacturing tips, trend forecasts, and company news to establish authority.
7. **Security**: Ensure all forms are secure and data is protected.
