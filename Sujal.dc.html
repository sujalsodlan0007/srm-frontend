Industry & Supply Chain
From Fabric To Finish.
Sujal garments move through a single, known line — cutting, sewing, finishing and quality control handled by partners we've visited and stayed close to. We don't outsource blind; we build relationships with the people making the clothes.

Our Story
Made By Hand, Owned By Us.
Sujal began with a simple frustration: clothes that looked considered but fell apart in a season, made somewhere nobody could name.
So we built the opposite — a small womenswear label that stays close to the line that makes it. We choose fabric first, fit second, trend never. Every collection is small enough that we can stand behind each piece in it.
Women First
Designed around how women actually move, sit and dress in a real day.
Full Transparency
We name our makers and open our process — see the Industry page.
Considered Pace
Fewer releases, held to a higher standard, meant to be kept.
Every stitch is placed by someone we've met in person.
